Interest under section 234C is 1% per month on the shortfall (rounded down to ₹100): three months for the first three instalments, one month for the last. No 234C interest on the first two instalments if at least 12% and 36% respectively has been paid. If less than 90% of the total is paid by 31 March, interest under section 234B runs at 1% per month from 1 April until the balance is paid. Estimates only; capital gains and other special-rate income are excluded.

The rules

Advance tax, in one minute.

  • Who: anyone whose tax for the year, after TDS and TCS, is ₹10,000 or more. Salaried people are usually covered by TDS; business owners, professionals, landlords and investors usually are not.
  • Exempt: resident senior citizens (60+) with no income from business or profession.
  • When: 15% by 15 June, 45% by 15 September, 75% by 15 December, 100% by 15 March. Cumulative, so each date is a running total.
  • Presumptive (44AD, 44ADA): the whole amount in one go, by 15 March.
  • Late: 1% per month under 234C for each instalment missed, and 1% per month under 234B from April if less than 90% is paid by 31 March. Late payment also shows up as interest on your ITR, so it is better to pay an estimate than nothing.
  • How to pay: e-filing portal → e-Pay Tax → Advance Tax (100), minor head 100, for the right assessment year.
