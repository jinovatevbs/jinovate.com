Services

The whole compliance lifecycle, handled.

Pick one service or hand us the entire finance function. Either way, you get structured, audit-ready output and a clear monthly picture of where things stand.

GST compliance and refunds

Monthly and quarterly returns, reconciliations and refund claims, with department notices answered on time.

  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9 and 9C
  • ITC reconciliation with GSTR-2B
  • E-invoicing and e-way bills
  • Refunds, including inverted duty structure
  • Notice and ASMT responses

Income tax and TDS

Returns, advance tax planning and TDS management for individuals, firms, LLPs and companies.

  • ITR-1 to ITR-7
  • Advance tax computations, quarterly
  • TDS and TCS returns: 24Q, 26Q, 27Q
  • Form 26AS and AIS reconciliation
  • Tax audits and assessment notices

Accounting, bookkeeping and MIS

Clean books you can hand to an auditor or a bank, and a monthly report you can actually read.

  • Bank and ledger reconciliations
  • Trial balance and ledger scrutiny
  • Cash-flow tracking
  • Monthly management reports
  • Year-end closing and audit support

Payroll and labour compliance

Monthly payroll run on schedule, with the statutory deductions filed where they belong.

  • Salary registers and payslips
  • TDS on salaries and Form 16
  • PF, ESI and professional tax
  • Joining and exit paperwork
  • Compliance trackers for growing teams

Company, LLP and ROC compliance

Incorporation through annual filings, with event-based forms handled as they come.

  • Private limited, LLP and OPC incorporation
  • AOC-4, MGT-7, Form 8 and Form 11
  • Director KYC (DIR-3 KYC)
  • DPIIT startup recognition
  • Board resolutions and event filings

Advisory, NRI and cross-border

Numbers for decisions, structures that hold up, and tax clarity for Indians living abroad.

  • Financial modelling and due diligence
  • Business restructuring and SOPs
  • NRI income tax and FEMA compliance
  • India–US cross-border tax
  • Net worth and other certificates

Questions

Before you get in touch.

Anything else, ask on WhatsApp.

What does it cost?
A fixed monthly fee, quoted in writing after the discovery call. It depends on the entity type, transaction volume and what is included, from GST-only to the full finance function. No hourly billing.
Can you take over mid-year, or if my books are behind?
Yes. We start with a catch-up: check what has been filed, bring the books up to date, clear any pending returns, and then move you onto the monthly rhythm.
Do you work with businesses outside Jaipur?
Yes. Most of the work happens over WhatsApp, email and a shared folder, so location does not matter. We work with clients across India and with NRIs abroad.
What do you need from me each month?
Bank statements, sales and purchase invoices, and any salary or team changes, by the 5th. We send the checklist on the 1st so nothing is missed.
How do I get my documents back?
A shared folder per client. Every filing, challan and acknowledgement goes there the day it is done, along with the monthly report, so you have a complete record at any time.
What can you do for NRIs?
Indian income tax returns, TDS on property sales, FEMA compliance for investments and property, repatriation paperwork, and tax questions that cut across India and the US.
